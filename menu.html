Sweet Questions, Answered

Our cotton candy is naturally gluten-free and vegan — it's just pure cane sugar and food coloring, spun fresh. Our classic funnel cakes contain wheat and eggs, but we're happy to point out options that fit your needs. Let our crew know about any allergies and we'll handle your order with extra care.

Yes! We accept cash, all major credit cards, Apple Pay, and Google Pay at the window. For larger catering bookings, we can invoice you directly with our payment terms.

Weekend dates fill up 6–8 weeks ahead during festival season, so we recommend booking early. For weekday private events, we can often squeeze you in with just a few days' notice. Contact us for our current calendar!

We're based in Metro City and regularly travel within a 75-mile radius. For festivals and multi-day events, we happily travel farther — travel fees may apply for long distances. Check our locations page for this week's schedule.
