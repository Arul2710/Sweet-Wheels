How It Started

From One Cone to a Whole Fleet of Smiles

In 2020, we loaded a secondhand cart into a pickup truck and spun cotton candy at a neighborhood block party. The line never stopped smiling — and neither did we.

Today we roll out of every spot with fresh cotton candy in a dozen flavors and golden funnel cakes hot from the fryer. Same recipes, same love, bigger wheels.

We're family-run, food-safe certified, and obsessed with turning ordinary afternoons into sugar-fueled celebrations.
