From Sugar to Cloud

How Fresh Cotton Candy Is Made

1

Pick Your Sugar

We load the machine with pure cane sugar in your chosen flavor & color.

2

Heat & Melt

The spinning head heats the sugar until it melts into a fine liquid.

3

Spin Into Fluff

Centrifugal force pushes the liquid through tiny holes, flash-cooling into silk.

4

Wrap & Serve

We wrap the cloud around a jumbo cone and top it off (if you like!).

2 minutes flat!
