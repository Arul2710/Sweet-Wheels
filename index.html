Our Story

A Family Business Built on Sugar & Joy

Sweet Wheels started in 2018 with one little truck, a secondhand cotton candy machine, and a big dream: to make every ordinary day feel like a carnival.

Today, we roll our pastel-painted truck all over the state, spinning cotton candy clouds and frying golden funnel cakes for fairs, festivals, weddings, and school events. No two stops are ever the same — and neither are the smiles.
